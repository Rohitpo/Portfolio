Datasheet · About

Profile

I'm an embedded systems engineer who works across the full stack of a hardware product — from GPIO-level firmware to the industrial data platforms, medical device UIs, and AI agents that sit on top of it.

By day, I build Qt-based medical device GUIs and OPC UA integrations for CNC and CMM machines at Bharat Electronics, turning factory-floor Ethernet data into quality monitoring and production reporting. My core toolkit is C/C++, Python, and Qt for firmware and industrial software, paired with wireless protocol work across WiFi, Zigbee, and BLE mesh from three years building smart-home firmware at Ayla Networks.

Recent independent work centers on Javious, a multi-agent AI agriculture system running on a Raspberry Pi 5 edge hub, coordinating six ESP32 modules across field sensing, market intelligence, and farmer-facing advisory agents — with local LLM inference so the system keeps working without reliable connectivity.

I care about systems that are reliable in the field, not just in the demo — real constraints like power budgets, protocol range, machine tolerances, and non-technical end users shape every design decision.

FLAGSHIP · IN PROGRESS

Javious — Multi-Agent AI Agriculture System

An edge-computing platform on a Raspberry Pi 5 hub coordinating field agents (soil, weather, crop health, irrigation), intelligence agents (market prices, government schemes, yield prediction), and advisory agents (fertilizer, harvest, storage, alerts) — all reachable by farmers through WhatsApp, a multilingual voice assistant, a web dashboard, or SMS fallback.

Six ESP32 modules (S3 / H2 / C3, two each) form a WiFi + Zigbee + BLE mesh feeding sensor data to the hub, where Ollama runs local LLM inference so the system keeps working without reliable internet — critical for rural deployment.
